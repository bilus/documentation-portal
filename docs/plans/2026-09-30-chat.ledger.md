- 2026-10-01: all 8 wrong implementations passed every test, unit and e2e.
- Decision, finding 1, the tools returned markdown that the page leaves out: applied. ReadDocument and Search use the text of the document page, which has no HTML comments, raw HTML or unused link definitions, and whose links point at the portal's pages. A document's title is its page's first level-one heading.
- Decision, finding 2, a conversation could not continue after ErrTooManyTools: applied. Ask stops after the tools answer the calls over the limit, so the history holds no call without its result.
- Decision, finding 3, the adapter dropped redacted_thinking blocks: applied. A thought part carries the block's data in its signature, after "redacted:", and goes back as redacted_thinking.
- Decision, finding 4, the rate limit: applied. admit no longer filters a client's records in place, a sweep forgets the questions older than the window and the clients without any, and an IPv6 client is its /64 network.
- Decision, finding 5, a conversation's history grew without bound: applied in part. A conversation that holds 512 KiB of messages and lookups takes no further question. One answer can still add 12 lookups. list_documents and list_operations stay uncapped: their size follows the documentation, which the operator controls, not the reader.
- Decision, finding 6, read_spec refused pointers that the other tools return, and aliases read differently by the spec's markers: applied. SpecPart indexes sequences and follows internal $refs, at most 8 hops, and the library always expands aliases.
- Decision, finding 7, autolinks opened in the chat's tab: applied. Images from any host: discarded, since the documentation is first-party and the reader's own question is the only other input. An answer that is one raw HTML block renders empty: deferred.
- Decision, finding 8, search order and titles: applied. When the guides and the spec both hold more matches than fit, the guides take half, rounded up. Titles as in finding 1.
