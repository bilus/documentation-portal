# Groups: list the portals under named groups

Ledger: `docs/plans/2026-11-26-groups.ledger.md`.
Design: `docs/flow.dfd`, `docs/flow.3.dfd`; terms in `docs/vocabulary.md`.
Branch: `bilus/development`, from `c4753c5`. No issue yet.

## Requirements

1. The configuration file may hold `groups:`, a list of groups, each with a `name` and `matchLabels`, a list of labels. `portal.ReadConfig` reads them into `portal.Config.Groups`, and a program may set `Config.Groups` in code. It refuses an empty entry, a key it does not know, and `matchLabels` that is not a list of strings.
2. A portal belongs to a group when its labels hold every match label of the group. A portal may belong to several groups or to none.
3. `portal.New` and `portal.NewLibraries` refuse, with an error that names the group: a group without a name, a group without match labels, an empty match label, and two groups of one name.
4. With groups, the home page lists the groups in their order, each as a heading with the links of its portals in the order of the portal configuration. A portal of two groups shows under both. A portal of no group is not listed.
5. With groups, the portal menu lists the same groups, each as a label over its portals' links, and holds no portal of no group.
6. A portal of no group keeps its pages at `/portals/{slug}/`. Its navigation bar shows the menu of the listed portals.
7. `portal.New` writes one line to the log for each portal of no group, and one for each group of no portal. `NewLibraries` writes none, so that docportal with a chat logs each line once for each build.
8. The home page and the menu count the listed portals alone: with one listed portal `/` redirects to it and no page has a menu, and with none the home page says that no portals are open.
9. Groups follow the reader's access: a group lists only its visible portals, and a group without a visible portal is left out.
10. Custom templates get the groups as `.Groups` on the home page and `.Nav.Groups` in the bar, each with `.Name` and `.Links`. `.Portals` and `.Nav.Menu` hold the listed portals, each once, so a template without groups still hides a portal of no group. The sample pages of the template check render groups.
11. `Library.Groups` returns the names of its portal's groups and `Library.GroupNames` every group's name, and the chat page's portal menu lists the portals as requirement 5 says.
12. Without groups, every page and every log is as it is today. The existing tests guard this.
13. The README, the vocabulary and the doc comments describe groups.

Out of scope: groups of sections; an access rule from a group; groups in the example application's demo.

## Questions and assumptions

- Q1. The match rule. Decided on 2026-11-26: a portal belongs to a group when it holds every match label. Match labels `a, b` match a portal labelled `a, b` or `a, b, c`, and not one labelled `a`, `b` or `x`.
- Q2. Items. Assumption: groups hold portals only. Sections keep their labels for the access hook.
- Q3. Where groups show. Assumption: the home page, the portal menu, and the chat page's menu (requirements 4, 5, 11).
- Q4. A portal of no group. Assumption: not listed, but still served at its URL (requirement 6). The groups shape the lists; the access hook decides who may read.
- Q5. Where the warning goes. Assumption: the log, at each build of the handler, so docportal repeats it at each reload that changes the snapshot, as it does for a toc problem.
- Q6. A group of no portal. Assumption: a warning in the log, since it is most likely a misspelt label, and the group is left out of the lists.
- Q7. A group without match labels would hold every portal. Assumption: refused as a mistake (requirement 3).
- Q8. The key is `matchLabels`, as you wrote it, although the other keys are lower-case words.
- Q9. Reviews. The defect review of AGENTS.md runs once, after the last stage's approval and before the pull request; its findings that need changes become stage 5.
- Q10. No issue exists for this change. Assumption: the pull request closes none, unless you open one.

## The change in brief

A group is a named set of portals: the portals whose labels hold every match label of the group. `ReadConfig` reads the groups, and `portal.New` puts each portal into its groups and writes a group problem to the log for a portal of no group and for a group of no portal. With groups, the home page and the portal menu link the listed portals under each group's name; a portal of no group is not listed and keeps its pages. Without groups nothing changes.

## Metaphor

A building's directory board. The board (home page, portal menu) lists tenants (portals) under department headings (groups) by the tags on each tenant's door (labels). A tenant with tags of two departments is on the board twice. A tenant on no heading is missing from the board, and its office stays open at its room number.
